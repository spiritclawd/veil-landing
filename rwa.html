Privacy Infrastructure for RWA Platforms

Veil enables tokenized asset platforms to protect investor data while meeting compliance requirements. Built for institutional deployment.

Why Veil

Compliance Without Exposure

The Challenge
RWA platforms face a dual problem: regulations require KYC and audit logs, but institutional investors refuse to participate if their positions and data are publicly visible on-chain.
Our Approach
Privacy-by-default architecture. Prove compliance without revealing underlying data. Keep investor identities, loan terms, and holdings confidential while satisfying auditors.
Capabilities

What Veil Provides

Private KYC Attestations
Investors prove accredited status without exposing personal documents or net worth details.
Transaction Obfuscation
Loan amounts, interest rates, and asset holdings are hidden while solvency proofs remain verifiable.
Audit-Ready Logs
Cryptographically signed compliance records available for regulator review without public disclosure.
Self-Custodial Design
No trusted third party holds secrets. Your users retain control of their private data.
