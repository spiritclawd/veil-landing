The Privacy Layer for Tokenized Assets

Veil enables RWA platforms to meet compliance requirements without exposing sensitive investor and originator data. ZK-powered privacy for the institutional on-chain economy.

The Problem

  • Regulatory risk: GDPR/CCPA exposure when investor KYC and asset data live on-chain in plaintext
  • Investor reluctance: High-net-worth individuals and funds won't participate if wallet history and positions are public
  • Competitive leakage: Loan terms, interest rates, covenants, and asset composition become visible to competitors

Our Solution

Veil is a drop-in ZK-privacy module for RWA platforms. Prove compliance without revealing data. Integration in weeks, not months.

ZK-KYC — Prove accredited investor status without revealing identity, net worth, or source of funds
Transaction Privacy — Hide loan amounts, interest rates, and asset holdings while proving solvency and collateralization
Audit Receipts — Generate cryptographically signed compliance records for regulators (future: anchor to Patronaige verification layer)
2-Week Integration — SDK + pre-built UI components; minimal changes to existing smart contracts
