Privacy Infrastructure for RWA Platforms

Veil enables tokenized asset platforms to meet compliance requirements without exposing sensitive investor and originator data. ZK-powered privacy built for institutional deployment.

The Problem

Compliance vs. Exposure

Regulatory Risk
GDPR/CCPA exposure when KYC and asset data live on-chain in plaintext.
Investor Reluctance
High-net-worth individuals and funds won't participate if positions are public.
Competitive Leakage
Loan terms, rates, covenants, and asset composition visible to competitors.
Solution

Veil for RWA

A drop-in ZK-privacy module for tokenized asset platforms. Prove compliance without revealing underlying data.

Capabilities
✓
ZK-KYC: Prove accredited status without revealing identity or net worth.
✓
Transaction Privacy: Hide loan amounts, rates, and holdings while proving solvency.
✓
Audit Receipts: Cryptographically signed compliance records (Patronaige integration planned).
✓
2-Week Integration: SDK + pre-built UI components; minimal smart contract changes.
Integration Pathway
Week 1: SDK sandbox + technical documentation review
Week 2: PoC for one core use case (e.g., investor accreditation)
Week 3: Testnet deployment + compliance officer walkthrough
Week 4: Mainnet integration + joint announcement
