Problem

Compliance Cannot Be Outsourced

RWA platforms rely on off-chain compliance providers that create single points of failure, data leakage risk and recurring fees. On-chain verification has been impossible at scale.

Off Chain Dependency
Traditional compliance providers hold user data, create custody risk and require constant renewal. Platforms cannot verify compliance without revealing sensitive information.
No On Chain Standard
Most protocols architecturally cannot embed compliance directly into the chain. Attestations remain off-chain, unverifiable and non composable.
Solution

Veil: On-Chain Compliance Layer

A self-custodial attestation protocol built for L2. Privacy by default, auditability on demand — no transfers, no custodians, no middlemen.

Self Custodial Attestations
Investors hold their own compliance proofs. No central provider stores data; attestations are anchored on-chain and owned by the user.
Privacy by Default
Attestation data remains private. Only the holder can selectively reveal it to auditors — a peephole model built on privacy primitives.
No Transfer Required
Compliance stays with the user account. RWA platforms verify attestations directly from the holder without needing to move assets or involve intermediaries.
On Chain Verifiability
Any auditor or platform can cryptographically verify compliance status on chain — no off-chain reconciliation, no trusted setup.
