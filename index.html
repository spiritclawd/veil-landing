Problem

On-Chain Exposure Kills Institutional Adoption

Public blockchains are transparent by default. That is a problem when tokenizing private credit, real estate or confidential loans.

Regulatory Exposure
GDPR and data residency laws can be violated when investor identity and transaction data are permanently public.
Investor Reluctance
HNWIs and funds will not participate if their positions or wallet activity are visible to competitors and the public.
Result

Platforms lose deal flow, face compliance risks and cannot scale to institutional volume. Privacy is not optional — it is the gatekeeper to capital.

Solution

Veil: Privacy-by-Default for RWA

A modular privacy layer that plugs into existing RWA smart contracts. No need to migrate assets or rebuild your platform.

Private KYC Attestations
Investors prove accreditation without revealing identity, net worth or jurisdiction. Zero-knowledge proofs accepted globally.
Transaction Confidentiality
Loan amounts, interest rates and holdings are hidden from public view while solvency remains mathematically provable.
Audit-Ready Receipts
Regulator-accessible compliance logs that can be shared with authorized parties without public disclosure.
Self Custodial Architecture
No trusted intermediary holds secrets. Private data never leaves the user device; proofs are generated client-side.
